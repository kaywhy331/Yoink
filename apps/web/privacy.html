Privacy policy · yoink-privacy-v1

Private plans, bounded browser evidence.

Yoink stores the minimum information needed to match exact products, enforce your limits, and explain what happened. Checkout, payment, addresses, cookies, credentials, and order details are outside the collection boundary.

What Yoink stores

Product and store identifiers, plan limits, normalized stock and seller observations, action states, user-reported outcomes and spend, pairing metadata, source health, and privacy-bounded diagnostics.

Chrome companion

The extension keeps pairing credentials, optional store permissions, short-lived cart evidence, delivery queues, and cart handoffs in Chrome storage. You can remove store permissions or delete all local extension data from Extension settings. A verified deletion receipt contains only the Chrome companion identity, current policy versions, and completion time.

Data Yoink does not collect

Yoink does not accept raw DOM, page text, screenshots, cookies, passwords, payment data, addresses, messages or member identities from Discord, or store order contents. The confirmation-page check only reads the page and stores only a bound action ID, store, confirmation page class, and timestamp.

Sharing and connectors

Community evidence is opt-in per eligible page and requires visible confirmation. User action connectors send only the selected Carted, Needs you, Missed, or Test event through a deployment-approved destination. Connector secrets and URLs remain deployment-owned and are not stored in user records.

Your controls

You can download your user data, pause actions, remove store permissions, disable update destinations, and delete Chrome companion data. Account deletion remains unavailable until hosted identity revocation and durable cross-store cleanup can be proven.
