Privacy policy · yoink-privacy-v1

Private plans, bounded browser evidence.

Yoink stores the minimum information needed to match exact products, enforce your limits, and explain what happened. Checkout, payment, addresses, cookies, credentials, and order details are outside the collection boundary.

What Yoink stores

Product and store identifiers, plan limits, normalized stock and seller observations (including what Chrome reads on store product pages you have open), saved Test call items, action states, user-reported outcomes and spend, pairing metadata, source health, and privacy-bounded diagnostics.

Chrome companion

The extension keeps pairing credentials, optional store permissions, short-lived cart evidence, delivery queues, and cart handoffs in Chrome storage. You can remove store permissions or delete all local extension data from Extension settings. A verified deletion receipt contains only the Chrome companion identity, current policy versions, and completion time.

Data Yoink does not collect

Hosted diagnostics do not accept DOM, page text, screenshots, cookies, passwords, payment data, addresses, messages or member identities from Discord, or store order contents. The confirmation-page check only reads the page and stores only a bound action ID, store, confirmation page class, and timestamp.

Local repair captures

In local mode, sanitized HTML evidence capsules are enabled by default. They may include product-page text, markup, store/product identifiers, adapter traces, and capture timestamps. When a store cart can't be read or a cart line can't be raised to your quantity, a capture of that store's cart page may also include the items, quantities and prices in that cart (at most one per store every 6 hours). Sanitization removes scripts, forms, input values, personal or account areas, cookies and credentials; these captures are different from the restricted hosted diagnostics described above.

Captures stay under the local Yoink data directory’s evidence folder, where the local operator and people with filesystem access can inspect them. They are not automatically uploaded to a shared repair service. Automatic captures expire after 30 days, manual captures after 90 days, and at most 50 per store are retained. Pruning runs when captures are ingested or when the operator runs the pruning command; expiry is not a scheduled deletion guarantee.

The operator can turn collection off in Admin → Settings → Store repair snapshots. Turning it off stops new collection; it does not erase saved files. To remove existing captures, stop Yoink and remove the evidence folder from its configured data directory. Deleting Chrome extension data does not delete these server-side local files.

Sharing and connectors

Community evidence is opt-in per eligible page and requires visible confirmation. User action connectors send only the selected In stock, Carted, Needs you, Missed, or Test event through a deployment-approved destination. Connector secrets and URLs remain deployment-owned and are not stored in user records.

Your controls

You can download your user data, pause actions, remove store permissions, disable update destinations, and delete Chrome companion data. Account deletion remains unavailable until hosted identity revocation and durable cross-store cleanup can be proven.

Change notice · {{PRIVACY_CHANGE_VERSION}}

This notice adds stock checks: while a supported store's product page is the tab in front, the Chrome companion reads that page's stock, price and seller, and Yoink keeps them only for items already in Yoink. Items you link for a Test call are kept with your max price and quantity until you remove them. It also covers cart-page repair captures: when a store's cart can't be read or a cart line can't be raised to your quantity, the sanitized cart page (its items, quantities and prices) may be captured, at most once per store every 6 hours. It also clarifies default-enabled, local-only sanitized HTML repair captures, retention, operator access and deletion controls. It also covers user-confirmed false-positive feedback, restart-safe local watch state, verified Chrome storage read-back, all-store confirmation-page classification, and sanitized health evidence. These additions do not collect store order details, expand the store action boundary, or permit checkout or order submission.
